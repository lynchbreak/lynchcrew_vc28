Day by day

Twenty-five days, chronologically, with stays, bookings, and the story behind each one.

Days 1–2

Hanoi

Hotel — La Siesta Classic Ma May, $190£101/night

A boutique luxury oasis nestled right in the pulsating heart of Hanoi’s historic Old Quarter.

Boasting a striking white colonial exterior and warm, Asian-inspired art deco interiors, this premium property provides a peaceful escape from the busy streets. Features an award-winning spa and the acclaimed Moonlight Sky Bar on the roof.

https://lasiestahotels.com/mamay

Activity — Hanoi Backstreet Scooter Tour, $85£45pp

An adrenaline-fuelled evening food crawl navigating Hanoi’s hidden alleyways on vintage scooters.

Safely guided by expert drivers, you will zoom past hidden landmarks, ride right along the operational train tracks, and stop at authentic family-run stalls to sample bun cha, egg coffee, and fresh banh mi.

https://hanoibackstreettours.com

Activity — Private City Highlights with WithLocals, $95£50pp

A fully customisable, privately guided cultural immersion through Hanoi’s heavy-hitting historic sites.

Skip the tour buses and explore the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum complex, the historic Temple of Literature, and the iconic Tran Quoc Pagoda alongside a local historian guide who tailors the pace to you.

https://withlocals.com

Activity — Thang Long Water Puppet Show VIP Seats, $15£8pp

Front-row VIP seats to a world-renowned ancient Vietnamese art form performed live on water.

Experience a beautiful century-old performance where traditional wooden puppets skip across a pool of water, accompanied by a live traditional operatic orchestra. These reserved front-row VIP seats guarantee the best view and acoustics away from the standard crowded general admission rows.

https://thanglongwaterpuppet.com

Days 3–4

Transfer — Luxury D-Car, $50£27pp

Cruise — Ambassador Signature Cruise, $450£239/night, meals incl.

Features massive private balcony suites, floor-to-ceiling windows, and fine dining. Avoids the standard tourist tracks of Halong Bay while taking you past dramatic karst formations, floating fishing villages, and hidden swimming spots. Includes premium return D-Car limousine transfers from Hanoi.

https://ambassadorcruise.com

Day 6

Hue

Hotel — Azerai La Residence Hue, $290£154/night

An enchanting 1930s French colonial governor’s mansion sprawling along the fabled Perfume River.

A living art deco museum boasting a massive saltwater pool, pristine tropical gardens, and stunning views of the Imperial Citadel. It offers an unmatched romantic atmosphere a step away from the central monuments.

https://azerai.com/azerai-la-residence-hue

Activity — Private Citadel & Royal Tombs Tour by Hue Smile Tours, $75£40pp

A private exploration of Vietnam’s ancient royal past across imperial palaces and ornate dynastic tombs.

Spend the afternoon uncovering the sprawling Forbidden Purple City and the architectural masterworks of the Khai Dinh and Tu Duc royal tombs with a specialised local guide and private driver.

https://huesmiletours.com

Days 7–11

Hoi An

Transfer — Private Hai Van Pass Car Transfer, $110£58 total

A scenic, private door-to-door drive from Hue to Hoi An over the world-famous coastal mountain pass.

Cruise over the dramatic peaks of the Hai Van Pass made famous by Top Gear, with custom stops for photos, lunch overlooking Lang Co Bay, and a visit to the Marble Mountains.

https://www.booking.com

Option 1 — Anantara Hoi An Resort, $500£265/night

Elegant French-colonial boutique luxury set directly on the banks of the scenic Thu Bon River.

Located less than a kilometre from the UNESCO World Heritage ancient quarter, featuring lush courtyard gardens, riverfront dining, split-level suites, and a premium day spa.

https://www.anantara.com/en/hoi-an

Option 2 — Airbnb, $350£186/night

Activity — Private Ancient Town Walking & Lantern Boat Cruise, $45£24pp

An atmospheric evening walking tour culminating in a private wooden boat cruise under thousands of lanterns.

Explore perfectly preserved Chinese assembly halls and the Japanese Covered Bridge before boarding a private wooden sampan boat to glide down the river and release floating paper lanterns.

https://withlocals.com

Activity — Red Bridge Cooking School Masterclass, $65£34pp

A hands-on gourmet culinary journey from a bustling local market straight to a riverside kitchen.

Includes a guided tour of Hoi An’s central market to select fresh herbs, a boat ride down the river to a secluded organic garden, and a masterclass where you cook iconic dishes like pho and crispy Banh Xeo pancakes.

https://redbridgevietnam.com

Activity — Golden Bridge & Ba Na Hills Excursion, $120£64pp

A private day trip up into the misty mountains to walk across the famous giant stone hands.

Includes a private car to the base stations, tickets for the record-breaking cable car system, and access to the iconic Golden Bridge walk before the afternoon crowds arrive.

https://sunworld.vn

https://www.thereveriesaigon.com

https://lesrivesexperience.com

Days 14–15

Mekong Delta

Transfer — Private VIP Limousine Transfer via Mui Ne Private Car, $160£85 total

A premium, private chauffeured VIP DCar Limousine transfer door-to-door from your Saigon hotel to your Can Tho island resort.

Travel in ultimate comfort inside a luxury vehicle custom-retrofitted with plush leather captain’s chairs, electronic device charge ports, and dual-zone air conditioning. Avoid the stress of local traffic as your professional driver handles the 4-hour highway journey to the Mekong Delta, including all road tolls and custom rest stops along the way.

https://muineprivatecar.net/tour-detail/saigon-to-cantho-by-private-car-25.html

Hotel — Azerai Can Tho, $340£180/night

A private, ultra-exclusive luxury eco-resort hidden away on a secluded islet in the Mekong Delta.

Accessible only by boat, this sprawling retreat features minimalist, high-end bungalows set under massive banyan trees, offering ultimate privacy and a world-class wellness centre.

https://azerai.com

Activity — Private Sunrise Cai Rang Floating Market Tour, $90£48 total

An early morning private boat excursion into the largest, most vibrant floating market in the delta.

Chartered directly by the resort, you will board a private wooden boat at sunrise to cruise among dozens of vessels trading local fruits and goods, enjoying fresh pineapple and hot noodle soup prepared right on the water.

https://azerai.com

A seamless, private overland transfer from Can Tho across the international border directly into Phnom Penh.

A dedicated private car and driver handle the full crossing via Tinh Bien, taking care of border formalities so you can settle in before arriving at the hotel.

https://www.booking.com

Hotel — Rosewood Phnom Penh, $280£148/night

A soaring, ultra-modern luxury sanctuary overlooking the Mekong River from the city’s iconic skyscraper.

Blends sleek contemporary design with subtle Khmer accents. Features the famous Sora sky bar jutting out over the city edge and an exquisite indoor pool.

https://rosewoodhotels.com

Days 17–20

Siem Reap

Flight — Flight 2, Saigon to Siem Reap, Vietnam Airlines, $240£127pp

Hotel — Angkor Village Hotel, $400£212/night

A tranquil, family-owned tropical garden retreat with authentic Khmer-style architecture in the heart of downtown Siem Reap.

With its lush tropical gardens, The Angkor Village Hotel is a tranquil oasis in the vibrant heart of downtown Siem Reap; the gateway to the UNESCO World Heritage Site. A small family-owned and operated property founded on authenticity and charm, the Hotel’s unique architecture is an homage to traditional Khmer style. Our 43 rooms, swimming pool, restaurant, theatre and wellness options were designed with respect for local culture, and nature.

https://angkorvillagehotel.asia/

Activity — Private Angkor Wat Sunrise Historian Tour, $110£58pp + pass

An unforgettable, privately guided sunrise exploration of the massive Angkor temple complex.

Led by a professional archaeological historian, witness the sun rise directly behind the towers of Angkor Wat before entering the ancient corridors early to beat the crowds. Covers Bayon and the root-strangled Ta Prohm temple.

https://angkorguides.com

Activity — Tonle Sap Lake Stilted Village Boat Cruise, $70£37pp

A private afternoon boat journey to witness local life in the stilted water-town of Kompong Phluk.

Board a private boat to explore this massive lake community where houses sit on towering 10-metre stilts, learning how local fishing families adapt to the dramatic seasonal water shifts.

https://angkorguides.com

Phuket

https://5starmarinephuket.com

An afternoon walking tour of vibrant Sino-Portuguese architecture ending with an elegant fine-dining dinner.

Stroll down the historic, pastel-coloured streets of Old Phuket Town before heading to the historic Governor’s Mansion for a multi-course Thai royal cuisine dining experience at Blue Elephant.

https://blueelephant.com

A fully licensed, premium private airport vehicle transfer from your resort straight to the HKT departure gates.

Conclude your trip with complete peace of mind. Sidestep local taxi negotiations by riding in a fully insured, legal, and air-conditioned private sedan or SUV operated by professional drivers, ensuring you arrive perfectly on time for your long-haul flight back home to Australia.

https://phuket.limo/
